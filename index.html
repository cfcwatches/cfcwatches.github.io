Sunpath

New Release

Sunpath is a clean, atmospheric watch face built around a dynamic daylight arc that traces the sun’s journey from sunrise to sunset. The arc updates in real time, showing daylight progress with sunrise and sunset positioned at each end for quick reference. As the day transitions into evening, the sun icon changes into a full moon, creating a natural visual cue for sunset and nightfall. Centered digital time ensures clear readability, complemented by a steps progress bar and three customizable circular stat fields. Sunpath includes ten visual themes, each crafted to harmonize with the daylight arc, moon transition, and overall layout.

Features

  • Dynamic daylight arc from sunrise to sunset
  • Real‑time daylight progress indicator
  • Sun icon transitions to moon after sunset
  • Sunrise and sunset times at arc endpoints
  • Centered digital time
  • Steps progress bar
  • Three circular stat displays
  • Smooth performance on AMOLED and MIP devices
  • 12‑hour and 24‑hour modes

Themes

  • Aqua
  • Ember
  • Arctic
  • Solar
  • Nebula
  • Mist
  • Twilight
  • Dawn
  • Forest
  • Midnight

On‑Device Settings

  • Theme selection
  • Choose any three stats from the full list
  • Show or hide the stat circles
  • Show or hide the date
  • Select date format (US or UK)
