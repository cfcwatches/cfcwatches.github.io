RedLine

New Release

Redline is a Performance Tachometer Watch Face, a premium AMOLED‑optimized watch face built around a clean motorsport‑inspired tachometer design. Custom bitmap time glyphs provide crisp, high‑contrast readability across all supported devices, while the sweeping heart‑rate rev gauge delivers dynamic color transitions and zone‑based status indicators. Choose from five striking themes Circuit Green, Carbon Blue, Ignition Orange, Voltage Purple, or Titanium Silver to match your gear.

Features

  • Dynamic Rev Gauge - Track your heart rate with a sweeping, zone-based gauge that transitions colors as you push your limits.
  • Crisp Readability - Custom bitmap time glyphs ensure your stats are sharp and legible, even at high speeds.
  • Engineered Style - Choose from five striking themes - Circuit Green, Carbon Blue, Ignition Orange, Voltage Purple, or Titanium Silver, to match your gear.
  • Fully Configurable - Three versatile stat fields let you monitor everything from VO2 Max and Recovery Time to Body Battery and Stress.

On‑Device Settings

  • Theme selection
  • Choose any three stats from the full list
  • Show or hide the stat fields
  • Show or hide sunrise/sunset

Sunpath

New Release

Sunpath is a clean, atmospheric watch face built around a dynamic daylight arc that traces the sun’s journey from sunrise to sunset. The arc updates in real time, showing daylight progress with sunrise and sunset positioned at each end for quick reference. As the day transitions into evening, the sun icon changes into a full moon, creating a natural visual cue for sunset and nightfall. Centered digital time ensures clear readability, complemented by a steps progress bar and three customizable circular stat fields. Sunpath includes ten visual themes, each crafted to harmonize with the daylight arc, moon transition, and overall layout.

Features

  • Dynamic daylight arc from sunrise to sunset
  • Real‑time daylight progress indicator
  • Sun icon transitions to moon after sunset
  • Sunrise and sunset times at arc endpoints
  • Centered digital time
  • Steps progress bar
  • Three circular stat displays
  • Smooth performance on AMOLED and MIP devices
  • 12‑hour and 24‑hour modes
